Put the product images in this folder.

Suggested file names:
  balsuite.png          (Balsuite - HRMS)
  balvirttechhub.png    (Balvirttechhub - training and careers)
  vahaan-tms.png        (Vahaan TMS - freight brokerage)
  layvora.png           (Layvora - poultry management)

Tips:
  - PNG, JPG or WebP, at least 1200px wide, landscape if possible.
  - Several images for one product are fine, e.g. layvora-web.png and layvora-mobile.png.
  - Do not include private customer names or data in screenshots.
